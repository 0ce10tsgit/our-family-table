Author Name

A bright, no-cook dish built on creamy butter beans instead of seafood — tossed in a tomato-lime dressing with red onion, jalapeño, and avocado. It's fresh, a little spicy, and comes together in a single bowl. Serve it cold with tortilla chips or piled onto tostadas.

Ingredients

  • Butter beans 2
  • Red onion 1
  • Jalapeño 2
  • Avocado 1
  • Lime juice 3
  • Olive oil 2
  • Salt 1

About the Author

Maria has spent the better part of three decades coaxing flavor out of whatever the season happens to offer. She learned to cook at her grandmother's elbow in a small kitchen where nothing was measured and everything was tasted, a habit she has never quite managed to break. After culinary school she worked the line at a handful of restaurants before deciding that the food she cared about most was the kind made for the people she loved, served at a crowded table on a slow Sunday afternoon. These days she develops recipes from home, testing each one until it is simple enough for a weeknight yet special enough to be worth remembering. She believes good food does not need to be complicated, only honest, and that the best dishes are the ones that get handed down and quietly improved by the next person to make them. When she is not in the kitchen she is usually in the garden, arguing with the tomatoes, or hunting through old cookbooks for ideas worth stealing. She hopes the recipes she shares here find their way onto your table and become, in time, a little bit yours.

Note from the Chef

This one comes together fast, so prep everything before you start — once the beans hit the dressing you'll want to serve it within the hour. If you like more heat, leave the jalapeño seeds in; for a milder bowl, scoop them out. Leftovers keep for a day, but the avocado is happiest the moment it's added.
